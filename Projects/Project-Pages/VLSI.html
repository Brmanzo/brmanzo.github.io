VLSI Sliding Filter Full Custom Layout

Sliding window filter circuit (Left) Custom circuit layout (Right)


For my final project in VLSI Design at UC Davis in 2023, we were tasked with implementing the sliding window filter circuit in full custom layout in 0.18 µm. Using Magic VLSI to design and validate the individual components, I arranged and connected the flip flops, subtractors and muxes in order to reduce the area and delay of the circuit. CMOS technology, and I was able to achieve an area of 24,291 µm² and a top-level delay of 6.61.

Custom placement layout and top level pin-out


Design considerations included regular spacing of dummy polysilicon, chains of inverters for the clock tree scaled to the input capacitance of the chip, and for the input pads scaled to the input capacitance of the pin.
